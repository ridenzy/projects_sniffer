provided here remain usable without payment, account registration, product
activation, usage metering, or a mandatory cloud service.

The MIT License permits commercial and noncommercial use, modification, and
redistribution under its terms. Paying for a service does not remove rights
already granted by the MIT License. Third-party dependencies retain their own
applicable licenses.

## Optional commercial work

The project may offer separately agreed professional work, including:

- **Custom analyzer development:** support for a language, technology, or
  specialized domain outside the current implementation.
- **Project analysis and setup:** help configuring scans, interpreting evidence,
  and integrating Project Sniffer into a development workflow.
- **Specialist analyzer or module packs:** separately delivered capabilities
  whose scope and license must be stated before purchase or delivery.
- **Professional or enterprise support:** agreed troubleshooting, maintenance,
  compatibility assistance, and response expectations.

These are the possible service categories.

## Ownership and delivery terms

Custom-work ownership, distribution rights, confidentiality, delivery scope,
and support obligations must be defined in a separate written agreement before
work begins. Do not assume that customer-specific work will automatically become
open source, exclusive, or transferable to the core repository without a prior
agreement.

Any separately distributed pack needs its own clear licensing information.
Nothing in such an agreement retroactively changes the existing MIT license of
Project Sniffer core code.

## Project name and branding

The MIT License covers the applicable software and documentation; it should
not be read as permission to present an unofficial fork, service, or product as
an official Project Sniffer release. See [Branding and name use](TRADEMARKS.md)
for guidance on official identity and third-party projects.

## Project boundaries

Official commercial work must not silently change the core's documented local-first,
read-only, non-executing analysis principles or turn uncertain relationships
into confirmed results. Features outside those boundaries require explicit
scope and safety review.
