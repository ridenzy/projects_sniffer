# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to participation in Project Sniffer's repository-managed
issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. It does not restrict lawful
private or commercial use of MIT-licensed software.

## Expected conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss technical decisions on their merits,
welcome good-faith disagreement, give useful feedback, and respect others'
time, privacy, and experience. Protect confidential project information.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted insults, stalking, doxxing, 
disclosure of private information without permission,
retaliation against people raising concerns, and persistent disruptive or
abusive behavior are not acceptable. Disagreement or technical criticism alone
is not misconduct.

## Raising a concern

For non-sensitive misconduct visible in public repository discussions, use the
repository's issue tracker when available. Refer only to already-public
material and do not include personal details or confidential evidence.

If a report requires confidential details, do not publish them in an issue,
pull request, or discussion. A verified private conduct-reporting channel for
Project Sniffer maintainers has not yet been published. 

GitHub's own abuse reporting controls, where available, are handled by GitHub and are not a
private reporting route to Project Sniffer maintainers.

Security vulnerabilities belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting is not a general conduct-reporting channel.

## Moderation and outcomes

Maintainers with the necessary repository permissions may request changes,
issue warnings, hide or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or
restrict participation. Actions should consider severity, context, and repeated
behavior. They may also refer platform-rule violations to GitHub.

Non-sensitive requests for reconsideration
may be raised through an appropriate repository issue. Do not publish private
reporting details to seek an appeal.

A verified private maintainer conduct-reporting contact remains a governance
requirement before confidential community reporting can be advertised.

For contribution rules, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
